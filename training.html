TRAINING & OWNERSHIP

Know your jet.

A practical guide to certification, fuel, maintenance, operating costs and the day-to-day realities of SF-50 ownership.

FUEL

Jet A

280 L tank

The SF-50 can fly for about five hours on a tank in the established operating profile. A typical fill is approximately 1 SD.

OPERATING COST

~100,000 credits

Budget approximately 100,000 credits per year for all operating costs in the current Sierra ownership estimate.

ENGINE

HT-1050J

The turbofan uses removable access panels, allowing maintenance work to be performed between flights when appropriate.

CERTIFICATION

Three months to certification.

SF-50 pilots begin with the aircraft type rating. Sierra provides simulator and systems training through Kingston, followed by instructor-led flying and the required checkride.

Simulator training can cover much of the certification process, but real-world instructor flights are recommended to build proficiency beyond the checkride.

BEFORE YOU FLY

Understand your operating environment.

RANGE & RESERVES

Plan around the 1,100 NM published range, weather, routing, reserves and actual passenger/cargo load.

AIRPORTS

Review runway length, obstacles, weight limits and local procedures before every trip. Blackstone has specific departure limitations.

MAINTENANCE

Keep the HT-1050J, landing gear, pressurization, flight controls, fuel system and VECTOR avionics on their required inspection and maintenance schedules.

DAILY OWNERSHIP

Plan for more than fuel.

Ownership includes inspections, scheduled maintenance, unscheduled repairs, insurance, airport/hangar costs, training, consumables and engine-related expenses. Sierra's 100,000-credit annual estimate is a planning figure rather than a guaranteed fixed cost.
