THE SF-50 STORY

It started with a simple question.

What if a personal jet didn't have to feel like a compromise?

The Sierra team set out to build an aircraft for pilots who wanted the speed and capability of a jet without giving up the flexibility that made general aviation special. The answer became the SF-50: compact enough for smaller airports, capable enough for business travel, and designed from the beginning around the pilot.

At the center of the aircraft is the HT-1050J turbofan. Around it, Sierra built a pressurized cabin, retractable landing gear and the VECTOR flight deck. The cabin remained modular because no two owners fly exactly the same mission.

Then came the first flight.

The aircraft lifted away from the runway and the idea became real. What had been drawings, parts and long hours on the production floor was suddenly an airplane in the sky.

Today, every SF-50 carries that same philosophy: give the pilot a capable aircraft and let the mission define the journey.
