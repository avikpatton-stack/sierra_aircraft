CABIN

Modular by design.

The cabin can be configured around the mission rather than locking the owner into one seating arrangement. Six-seat, four-seat and two-seat layouts can be used depending on passengers and cargo needs.

SYSTEMS

Built for everyday ownership.

Pressurization, retractable landing gear, working cabin ventilation and a single HT-1050J turbofan give the SF-50 the systems expected of a modern light jet.

AVIONICS

VECTOR flight deck.

Integrated PFD and MFD information keeps the cockpit focused on the pilot, navigation and aircraft systems.

FIELD OPERATIONS

Compact airport footprint.

The SF-50 is designed for short-field missions. At Blackstone, it can operate toward the ocean side because of the 50-foot obstacle on Runway 27, and departures from Blackstone are limited to 60% of maximum load.
